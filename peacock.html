Honest read. This is roughly 40% of the reference. What's missing:
  1. Tail is a fan, not a cascade. Mine radiates from one point; the reference drapes with no visible origin.
  2. Eyes are flat rings — identical concentric ellipses, stamped on. Reference eyes are graded and each one differs.
  3. Feathers are spikes, hard-pointed. Reference feathers are soft-edged with barb texture.
  4. Body reads as clipart — blue teardrop plus gold blob, versus a sculpted neck and layered coverts.
  5. No true relief. One drop-shadow per group, not per element.

Closing that gap is ~1,500 hand-placed beziers and days of work, shipping 300KB+ of SVG. That is the evidence for Route B in hero-spec.md §6: peacocks and foliage as raster, cartouche and wordmark as live SVG.
